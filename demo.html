Lesson debrief

Tuesday, September 15 · 60 minutes

Repertoire worked

  • "Deep River" (Burleigh): worked from "I want to cross over" with the warm quality leading. The low F entrances got the open-open onset treatment; the descent kept its brightness instead of going smoky.
  • "She Used to Be Mine" (Waitress): bridge only. The build stayed on airflow instead of squeeze through "she is gone, but she used to be mine."

Exercises

  • Marmalade descents: bright and buzzy on the way down, small space, no dome.
  • Open-open onsets: breathe in, close, then open. The onset is a release, not a push.
  • Straw slides: through the passaggio, spigot on the whole way.
"That descent kept every bit of its ring. That is the version we keep." Prof. G, in the lesson
